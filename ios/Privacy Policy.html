Privacy Policy

Iduio for iOS · Last updated November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Iduio (the “App”), published by Code Solix, handles information on iOS. We built Iduio to work on your device: we do not require an account and we do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

Your audio files are processed locally on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers.

1. Information We Access on iOS

  • Audio and video files you select to convert, trim, merge, compress or play. They stay on your device.
  • Recordings you make with the in-app recorder. They are saved only in the App’s library on your device.
  • App settings such as theme, language and library items, stored locally on your device.
  • Purchase status. If you subscribe to Premium, the payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no card or billing details; the App Store only tells the App whether Premium is active.

2. How We Use the Information

Only to provide the features you ask for: converting, editing, recording, playing, sharing and saving audio, and unlocking Premium features you purchased. We do not use your files for advertising, profiling or analytics.

3. Data Storage & Sharing

  • Files and settings live in the App’s private storage on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App.
  • Files leave the App only when you choose to share or save them, using the system share sheet or save-to-files dialog.
  • We use no advertising SDKs and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • The App makes small network requests to load our legal pages, to check whether a newer version exists, and to communicate with the App Store for purchases. These requests contain no personal content from your files.

4. Permissions Summary (iOS)

PermissionWhy
MicrophoneOnly when you tap Record, to capture audio you choose to save. iOS asks you first, and you can turn it off in Settings › Privacy › Microphone.
Files you pickThrough the system file picker, Iduio can only read the files you select. Iduio does not request photo library or contacts access.
Background audioLets playback continue with the screen locked and shows lock-screen controls. No data is collected.

5. Children’s Privacy

Iduio is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. Since the App collects no personal data, there is nothing to delete from our side.

6. Your Choices

  • Revoke microphone access at any time in your device settings.
  • Delete recordings and library items inside the App, or uninstall the App to remove all local data.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID subscriptions (Settings › your name › Subscriptions).

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the App changes. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version. Continued use after an update means you accept it.
