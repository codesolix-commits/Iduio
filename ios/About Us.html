About Iduio

Iduio is a fast, private audio toolbox for iOS. Convert between formats, trim and merge tracks, compress files, record audio and pull the sound out of videos, all on your device, with no account needed.

  • Convert audio to popular formats
  • Trim and merge tracks
  • Compress to save space
  • Record with the built-in recorder
  • Extract audio from video
  • Library, history, playback with lock-screen controls
